Questions people ask before they trust this

  • How do you decide a theme is strong?

    By counting. Each theme shows how many feedback items sit behind it and which sources they came from. When the count is low the theme is labelled low signal, and you can still open every item.

  • What happens to personal details?

    Names, addresses and contact fields are scrubbed before anything is stored, on every source, by default. On a CSV you also choose which columns are read at all.

  • What if the AI gets a theme wrong?

    Every claim carries the citations it was built from, so you can check it in seconds rather than take it on trust. A claim that cannot be traced is removed, not smoothed over.

  • Who can see a brief I share?

    Anyone with the link, without logging in. The dialog says so before you copy it, and you can disable the link at any time, which stops it working for everyone.
