How to use it

When

When one decision about one thing needs the whole attention, and the person can finish it or step back. It floats, so it is the one surface besides the frame that carries the shadow, and it takes the frame's radius so the two read as the same material.

When not

  • To say something about a screen that still works: take the notice band.
  • To put the destructive action beside the primary one: it is held apart by .u-apart.

Limits

Usage rules that name this component. Each is written once, on Architecture, the usage rules, with where it was taken from; this is the short copy.

  • R1. At most one filled .btn visible on a screen; every other real action takes .btn--ghost. An action at rest and an action on selection are exclusive in CSS: a ticked theme card shows .selbar and hides .head-action. On 0.0 Home the count is per section.
  • R5. A .notice--warn or .notice--error never sits on a raised surface such as .dialog: its ink falls under 4.5 there.
