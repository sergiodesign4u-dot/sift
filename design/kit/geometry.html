Geometry

Space shown as space, radius shown as a corner, a target shown at its own size. Geometry gets no semantic level by rule: a component reads it straight from primitive, because a theme has nothing to override in a corner.

Space

Shown as space rather than as a list of numbers. Eleven steps, and they are the ones the product measured out on real screens: a scale invented as a ratio would have produced steps nothing uses and left out the ones everything does.

Targets

44px is the floor for anything a thumb aims at, and it has exactly three named exceptions, all of them in wireframes/docs/conventions.md: the checkbox at the 24px AA floor, because a 44px checkbox would double the height of every ranked row; an inline link inside running text, exempt for the reason WCAG exempts it; and the citation chip, which is the same case.

--size-target
44px
--size-check
24px, named exception
--size-bar
52px, the app bar

The avatar is the case worth remembering: it READS at 28px and is HIT at 44 through a transparent overlay. The visual and the target may differ, and where they do the component page says so.

The page frame

The product is mobile-first and reasoned from the desktop: one live screen that narrows, never a desktop mock beside a mobile mock. Every block has to survive 360px, and that is a hard gate this project has checked at every stage.
