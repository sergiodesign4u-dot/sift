The top bar of every signed-in screen, shown alone at real scale. In place it is the first zone of the screen shell: see it in a frame on Screen shell, or on 2.0 Synthesis.

Variants and states

The states belong to each tab: rest, hover, focus and current. Hover moves the ink only; current is primary ink on a raised ground, marked by tone and never by the accent. There is no pressed tab and no disabled tab, because app-header.css paints neither. The bar itself is not interactive: its content changes with the screen, and on an account screen no tab is current, because the account is the avatar in the corner. The avatar keeps its own states on Avatar.

How to use it

When

At the top of every signed-in screen, as the first zone of the screen shell. Canonical global 0.1: its skeleton is defined once, in design/kit/shell.html, and copied from there.

Three entries plus account. Synthesis is the default landing, then Sources and Briefs. The account sits in the corner as the avatar. Three, not five, is the calm-under-density choice.

When not

  • For a screen read by somebody who is not signed in (0.0, 6.4, 9.x): take the public bar. A shared brief is a read-only artifact with no navigation to offer.
  • Below 37.5rem (600px): the tabs give way to the bottom tabs, which add Account as a fourth destination.
  • For the way back inside a section: take the breadcrumb, the way back in one line.

Limits

Usage rules that name this component. Each is written once, on Architecture, the usage rules, with where it was taken from; this is the short copy.

  • R8. .appbar and .bottomtabs only inside the signed-in app, and always together; .public-bar only on the public pages. The two never meet.

Behaviour at width

Shell form A, Stage 10 Step 3. Mobile first: below --bp-shell-wide (37.5rem, 600px) the bar holds the brand and the avatar, and the bottom tabs carry the sections. From 37.5rem the three text tabs appear and the bar becomes sticky, because there it is the navigation carrier and the IA's state "Scrolled: navigation is sticky and persists" (ia/docs/pages/navigation.md:35) belongs to the carrier; --layer-shell keeps it above the page. Why this form: three entries and no persistent second level, so the entries go into the header, not a rail. The hidden tabs are display: none, out of the accessibility tree too: exactly one carrier at every width, checked by computed style on 410 frames from 320 to 1600 and by a Tab walk. The ground runs the full width; the brand and the avatar stand over the page column (--frame-inset, Screen shell). This is one of the two legal @media of a component, and its number is the register's literal: a media query cannot read var(). Both forms live on Responsive.
