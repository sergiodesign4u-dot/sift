Skeleton bar

The skeleton is shaped like what arrives, so the wait promises the right thing. What is already known before the wait is shown as itself and never as a grey bar.

design/system/components/skeleton.css · 2.2 and 4.2.

Variants

AxisValuesThe rule that chooses
widtha · b · cUneven, on purpose. Equal bars read as a table; uneven ones read as lines of prose, which is what is coming
shapegeneric · shaped like the real rowWhere the arriving thing has a distinctive shape, the skeleton takes it. On 2.2 the skeleton has the checkbox and rank columns of the theme card, so the wait promises what arrives

When to use it

Only where the product is waiting for something it can describe. On 2.2 the synthesis is running and the skeleton carries the shape of the ranked list; on 4.2 the evidence is opening and the skeleton carries three lines per item grouped by source.

What it never covers is a number the product already has. The count, the recency and the rank are printed as themselves while the items load, because a grey bar over a fact the product already knows is a lie about how much it knows.

Rule and anti-rule

rule
42 items from 2 sources. Opening the items and their citations.

What is known is text. Only what is loading is a bar.

anti-rule

Everything greyed, including the head. The screen now claims to know nothing, and the person cannot tell whether the count is loading or gone.

If you are about toTake insteadWhy
show that a background job is runningthe phase stripA phase strip names the steps. A skeleton stands where content will be
show that something failedthe state block, or .noticeA skeleton that never resolves is the worst error state there is
fill an empty screenthe empty state blockEmpty is not loading, and the difference matters to a person who just connected a source

States

No states, and that is a decision rather than an omission. A skeleton is a placeholder. Its one behaviour is the shimmer, and that stops entirely under prefers-reduced-motion, which base.css handles for the whole product in one rule.

Four states are given to a component because it is interactive, not because a template has four cells. Faking one with a class the product does not carry (.is-hover) would be the same defect as an invented role.
