Variants and states

Not interactive: no hover, focus or pressed state. A skeleton is a placeholder. Its one behaviour is the shimmer, and that stops entirely under prefers-reduced-motion, which base.css handles for the whole product in one rule: animation: none !important. This page does not fake the reduced-motion state with an inline style, so to see it, switch the system setting and reload: the bars then stand still.

How to use it

When

Only where the product is waiting for something it can describe. On 2.2 the synthesis is running and the skeleton carries the shape of the ranked list; on 4.2 the evidence is opening and the skeleton carries three lines per item grouped by source.

What it never covers is a number the product already has. The count, the recency and the rank are printed as themselves while the items load, because a grey bar over a fact the product already knows is a lie about how much it knows.

When not

  • To show that a background job is running: take the phase strip. A phase strip names the steps. A skeleton stands where content will be.
  • To show that something failed: take the state block, or .notice. A skeleton that never resolves is the worst error state there is.
  • To fill an empty screen: take the empty state block. Empty is not loading, and the difference matters to a person who just connected a source.

Four states are given to a component because it is interactive, not because a template has four cells. Faking one with a class the product does not carry (.is-hover) would be the same defect as an invented role.
