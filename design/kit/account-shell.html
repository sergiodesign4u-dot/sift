How to use it

When

On the four account screens, 7.0 to 7.3: the same nav and the same panel on all of them, so the place you are is always the same shape. The panel is where the account rows live.

The nav sits above the panel when there is no room and beside it from 821px (kit.css, "Account shell"). The current item is marked by ink, ground and strip together, never by colour alone.

When not

  • For the product's three destinations: those are Synthesis, Sources and Briefs in the app header. Account sits in a corner menu, not in a nav of its own.
  • For a list of links that go somewhere with a sentence each: take the row link, cardrow.html.
  • For one setting with a switch: take the account row.
