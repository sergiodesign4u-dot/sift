No colour screen carries this molecule yet. The copy is from the grey screens in wireframes/, and the screens are painted at Stage 12 (docs/decisions.md, accepted: "Auth card, three forms"; design/kit/docs/inventory.md). The grey fields and buttons are the system's atoms here: Input, Button, Field label, Inline text link. The fields stand in .field inside .form, which space a label, its control and its error, as on every other form of the product. The heading is .auth-h2 because the card sits under the screen's own title; the grey 1.0 sets its title as an h1 and the system has no title form for the card yet, which is a backlog row for Stage 12.

Variants and states

Not interactive: the card has no hover, focus or pressed state of its own. The rows are content forms. The inputs, buttons and links inside keep their own states on their own pages. The inventory lists the states "rest, submitted": submitted is the sent confirmation below, a content form, and the css paints no separate submitted state.

How to use it

When

On the logged-out screens 1.0 to 1.2: log in, sign up, reset. The card is the sheet the form lives on, and it is also the sheet for the confirmation that follows a request.

Every state names the next move (CLAUDE.md, voice). The confirmation says what was sent and when the link expires, and the switch line gives the other way in.

When not

  • Inside the application: the signed-in screens are not on a sheet.
  • For a failure that blocks work: take .notice. The confirmation inset is quiet and reports a request, not an error.
  • For a line inside a sentence: take .link.

If an account exists for that email, we have sent a link to reset your password. Open it to continue. The link expires in 30 minutes.

Do
Say what happened and what comes next
The link, and when it stops working. The voice rules ask every state to name the next move.

Success! Your email was sent successfully.

Do not
Celebrate and name nothing
The voice rules forbid "successfully", exclamation marks and celebration, and the line says nothing about what to do next.
