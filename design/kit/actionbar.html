How to use it

When

At the end of a form or a step, where the screen asks for its one decision. On 3.2 the note says what is still missing, so the person reads the reason beside the button that is not ready, rather than finding it above.

When not

  • To hold two filled buttons: a screen has one primary action, and the footer does not compete with it.
  • To hold a selection: the selection bar is a different molecule.
