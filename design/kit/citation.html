When to use it

On every claim the synthesis makes. This is the component that makes the fifth design principle real: traceable or it does not exist. A summary sentence without one is a sentence this product is not allowed to print.

It carries the SOURCE and the COUNT rather than just a number, which is a deliberate difference from an academic footnote: "Intercom +11" tells a sceptical reader both where the evidence came from and how much of it there is, before they decide whether to open it.

Rule and anti-rule

rule

Several accounts abandoned setup entirely Intercom +4.

One clause, one citation, and the citation names its source and its size.

anti-rule

Several accounts abandoned setup entirely 1 2 3.

Three bare indices. They say a claim has support without saying from where or how much, which is the shape of a footnote rather than of evidence.
