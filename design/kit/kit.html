Stage 07 · UI + Visual · Step 3

Component kit

Fifty components, three levels, one flat stylesheet. A screen is not drawn here, it is assembled: every product screen from Step 5 on takes its classes from kit.css and carries no style of its own. Something missing goes into the kit first and onto the screen second.

The values did not start in this folder. Stage 06 wrote them by hand from the approved brand plate into design/_theme.css, and this step moved that file here with git mv, the :root block byte for byte, so the line from a pixel on the plate to a token in the design system is never broken by a re-derivation. The thirty-five screens that had never been in colour brought their structure from their own wireframe and their appearance from the language, and nothing else.

Open for your decision, before this kit is used to build anything

Nine values entered :root in a block marked PENDING. Six of them were already in the file, written as literals inside a class at Stage 06, which is the exact drift a kit exists to close: one hairline appeared as #333c44 in one rule and #3a444c in four others, and --gunmetal was referenced by the checkbox without ever being declared. Two are new (--red-wash, --red-edge), built the same way as the amber pair, because --red was declared at Stage 06 and not used by a single rule, and a blocking failure needs a surface. One (--r-lg) is the frame radius, so a dialog can be the same material as the frame.

  1. --line-2: #3a444c, the second hairline. The two hover tones folded to one.
  2. --cyan-wash and --cyan-edge, the accent as a surface and an edge.
  3. --amber-wash and --amber-edge, low signal and warning.
  4. --red-wash and --red-edge, blocking failure only. New.
  5. --shadow, the one depth in the product, and --r-lg: 12px, the frame radius.

Geometry is still written as literals inside classes (font sizes, paddings, the type scale). That is how the file arrived and it is not fixed here on purpose: naming those is the primitive and semantic token split, which is Stage 08. Said out loud so it is a decision and not an oversight.

Beside each value is its origin, taken from the comment that travels with the variable in kit.css: plate B pixel means it was read off the approved brand plate at Stage 06, attribute means it was derived from one of the five signed attributes, taste and technical decision mean what they say. Form and motion

Three radii and one easing. A radius is chosen by what the hand does with the thing: 4px for a mark that sits inside text, 6px for anything a hand acts on, 12px for a sheet that floats above the screen.

--r-sm 4px
--r 6px
--r-lg 12px
--shadow

--ease: cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1), ease out and restrained. Every transition in the kit runs on it, and every one of them is switched off under prefers-reduced-motion.
