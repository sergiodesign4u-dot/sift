The scale

Ten steps, and each one is here because the product renders it, not because a ratio produced it. Every specimen is real product text from voice/docs/microcopy.md: a scale shown on lorem ipsum hides exactly the problem it should reveal, which is what a real line looks like at that size.

The scale is --type-* and not --text-*. That rename happened while the token file was being written: --text- has to mean ink and nothing else, or the surface rule stops being greppable.
