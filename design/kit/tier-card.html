Variants and states

Not interactive: no hover, focus or pressed state. A tier card is a block of information. Its buttons and links, "Start free" and "See plans and limits", sit below the grid and are atoms (Button). The rows are the plans, the one axis the inventory names: value, one per plan.

How to use it

When

On the marketing home, one card per plan, with the price as the largest thing in it. The price is a number, so it is set in mono.

When not

  • To hold the action: the buttons sit below the grid on 0.0, not in the card.
  • To fix a price: the plan names, limits and prices are early hypotheses (design/index.html).

Anatomy

One card per plan, with the price set in mono. The bullet is a fill, so it reads a component token and not a line role.

ZoneClass or elementRule
the card.tierA column on the surface, hairline edge, medium radius
the name.tier-namePrimary ink, strong weight
the price.tier-priceMono, the largest thing in the card
the unit.tier-unitMono, secondary ink, optional
the features.tier-featA grid list with a 5px square bullet before each item

Axes, with the rule that chooses

AxisValuesThe rule that chooses
valueone per planThe inventory says three tiers (Level 2 table); design/index.html carries four: Free, Starter, Growth, Enterprise. The count is [?] until the owner settles it

No states, and that is a decision rather than an omission. The card is a block of information and has nothing to press.

Tokens it reads

ReadsGrows fromFor
--bg-surface, --line-hairline--grey-900, --grey-800the card ground and edge
--text-primary, --text-secondarytokens.cssthe name and the price; the unit and the features
--tier-mark--grey-700 dark, --grey-350 lightthe bullet, a FILL. kit.css drew it in --line-2, which became --line-control, a line role, and a fill may not borrow a line role. Accepted at Step 6
--radius-mdtokens.cssthe corner
--font-mono, --type-base, --type-xl, --type-2xs, --type-xs, --weight-strong, --weight-mediumtokens.csstype for each zone
--space-3, --space-4, --space-6, --space-8tokens.csspadding and rhythm

Kept as literals because no primitive equals them: 2px under the price, 15px bullet indent, 1.5 line height on the features, and the 5px bullet with its 7px offset and 1px corner.
