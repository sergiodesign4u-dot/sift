Evidence · 42 items

When to use it

Wherever a list has more than one kind of thing in it: connected sources against available ones, shared briefs against drafts, evidence grouped by source. It is the smallest structural signal in the product and it does a lot of work on dense screens.

The empty group is the case that matters. On 3.4 and 6.5 the product shows the group heading with a zero and an empty block under it, rather than hiding the group: the gap is visible instead of assumed, which is the same rule the empty CSV group on 4.1 follows.

Rule and anti-rule

rule
CSV · 0 items

No CSV item mentions Salesforce. The group is shown so the gap is visible rather than assumed.

A group with nothing in it still gets its heading, its count and one line saying so.

anti-rule
Connected sources

A group heading promoted to a real heading. It now competes with the screen H1, and on a screen with four groups there are five things claiming to be the title.

States

No states, and that is a decision rather than an omission. A group heading is a label. It is not focusable, not pressable, and giving it a hover would make a person try to click a word that leads nowhere.

Four states are given to a component because it is interactive, not because a template has four cells. Faking one with a class the product does not carry (.is-hover) would be the same defect as an invented role.
