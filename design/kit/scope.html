When to use it

Under the head of every screen that ranks, counts or filters anything. It is the sentence that turns a list into a claim somebody can check, and in this product that is the difference between a synthesis and a summary.

Its hardest job is the stale case. On 3.5 a source has failed, and the honest line is not "something went wrong": it is that the ranking is counted from the same corpus as two days ago, which tells the person exactly how much to trust what they are looking at.

Rule and anti-rule

rule

42 items from 2 sources: Intercom 30, CSV 12. First seen 5 weeks ago, latest 2 days ago.

The size, the split by source, and the age. Three facts that let a person judge the claim without opening anything.

anti-rule

Showing the most relevant themes from your feedback.

A sentence with no number in it. It says the ranking exists and nothing about what it is counted from, which is the one thing the line is for.

States

No states, and that is a decision rather than an omission. The scope line is a sentence. Its four states are content states, written in words by whoever owns the screen, not interaction states.

Four states are given to a component because it is interactive, not because a template has four cells. Faking one with a class the product does not carry (.is-hover) would be the same defect as an invented role.
