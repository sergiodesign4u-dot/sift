List row link

A row that IS the link: the whole strip is the target, not a word inside it. It is kept apart from the six other row-shaped components in this product because its zone set is different, and a zone that is gone means a different component rather than a variant.

When to use it

A hub that lists destinations rather than data. On 7.0 it is the three sections of the account, each with the one line that tells the person whether they need to go there at all. That line is why the row exists in this form: a bare list of three words would make the person open all three to find out.
