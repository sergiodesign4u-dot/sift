How to use it

When

A hub that lists destinations rather than data. On 7.0 it is the three sections of the account, each with the one line that tells the person whether they need to go there at all. That line is why the row exists in this form: a bare list of three words would make the person open all three to find out.

When not

  • To list data the person will act on: take .src-row, .brief-row or .theme. Those rows carry status, actions and evidence. This one carries a destination.
  • To put two actions in the row: take a row with buttons. A row that is entirely a link cannot hold a second target inside it.
  • To link inside a sentence: take .link.

Anatomy

A row that IS the link: the whole strip is the target, not a word inside it. It is kept apart from the six other row-shaped components in this product because its zone set is different, and a zone that is gone means a different component rather than a variant.

ZoneClass or elementRule
the row.cardrowThe anchor itself. The whole strip is the target, so there is no small link to aim at
the titlestrong.cardrow-tPrimary ink, one line
the linespan.cardrow-dSecondary ink. It says what is behind the row, with a number where there is one
the ruleborder-bottomBelongs to the row and not to a list wrapper, so a row can stand alone

Axes, with the rule that chooses

AxisValuesThe rule that chooses
no axesOne form. Everything that varies is its content
hoverthe only stateThe ground lifts by one step. On a strip with no border and no fill at rest, the hover is the only thing saying the whole row is pressable
