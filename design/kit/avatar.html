When to use it

In the corner of the app bar, as the way into the account, and once at 40px on the account screen itself. The navigation of this product is three entries plus account, and the account lives in a corner precisely because account work is not the job the product is for.

The empty content cell is the interesting one. A product whose promise is that every claim traces to a real customer cannot decorate itself with invented people, and the same rule that keeps stock photography out of the interface keeps a generated face out of this disc. Where a SOURCE needs an identity, it is a channel token: the icon slot.

Rule and anti-rule

rule
28px, and the hand hits 44

The visual stays quiet in a bar full of navigation, and the target still clears the floor.

anti-rule
the accent spent on a corner

A coloured avatar. The one accent of the product now sits in the corner of every screen, competing with the primary action it is supposed to mark.
