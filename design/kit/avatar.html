Variants and states

A monochrome disc, and it stays one. This product scrubs personal data by default, so a face here would be the same lie as a face on a piece of evidence. Size is given by the place: two sizes, and one content value, which is empty. Two states, and one of them is deliberately empty: hover paints nothing, and focus-visible is the product ring.

How to use it

When

In the corner of the app bar, as the way into the account, and once at 40px on the account screen itself. The navigation of this product is three entries plus account, and the account lives in a corner precisely because account work is not the job the product is for.

The empty content cell is the interesting one. A product whose promise is that every claim traces to a real customer cannot decorate itself with invented people, and the same rule that keeps stock photography out of the interface keeps a generated face out of this disc. Where a SOURCE needs an identity, it is a channel token: the icon slot.

When not

  • To show which source an item came from: take .iconslot. A channel token, not a person.
  • To show a real person: take nothing. There is no such component, and there will not be one while feedback authors are scrubbed.
  • To make the corner more prominent: take nothing. The corner is quiet on purpose. Account is not the job.
