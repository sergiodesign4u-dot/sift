The sizes the product uses, and the rule under them

An icon has no size of its own. Every slot in the system sizes its own glyph, and the one place that did not cost this product a screen: an svg with no width is 300x150 by specification, so a drag handle took 300px of a 316px row and pushed the composer off a 360 viewport. Found in the browser at Stage 07 Step 5, invisible in the file.

The glyph takes its ink from currentColor, so an icon is never a colour decision of its own: it is whatever ink the thing around it is already using. That is why the icon roles are the ink roles and there is no --icon-* anywhere in the system.
