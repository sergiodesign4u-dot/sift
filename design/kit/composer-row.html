How to use it

When

On 6.1 and 6.2, where a PM orders the themes of a brief. The order is the recommendation, which is why the handle comes first and the rank is shown beside it.

When not

  • For a saved brief in a list: take the saved brief row. It has a status and one way in, and no handle.
  • For the ranked theme in the synthesis: that is the theme card, which has a checkbox and a quote.
  • The inventory keeps these as three components and not variants of one, because the zones differ.
