Variants and states

The row has no state of its own. Its second form carries a checkbox and the row is the label, so the one state is the checkbox's own, checked or not, drawn by the atom. Hover and focus on the checkbox are on checkbox.html and are not repeated. The label form has a pointer cursor and no hover ground, because the css paints none.

How to use it

When

A setting of the account, one per row, with the sentence that says what it does. When the setting is an on and off choice, the row takes the checkbox and becomes the label, so the person has the whole strip to aim at and not a 24px box.

When not

  • To link to a section of the account: take the list row link. This row holds a setting, not a destination.
  • To list data the person acts on: take a source row or a brief row.
Do
The whole row is the label
A click anywhere on the strip toggles the setting. The sentence is part of the control.
Scrub PII by defaultNames and email addresses in imported feedback are scrubbed on ingest. On by default for every source.
Do not
A checkbox in a row that is not a label
Now the 24px box is the only target, and the sentence next to it does nothing when it is clicked.
