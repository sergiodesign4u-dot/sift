How to use it

When

When a view has nothing to show, or could not show it, and the person needs to know why and what to do next. Take the canvas when the whole screen is the empty or the failed thing. Sit inline when the screen is otherwise populated and the next action is further down: the empty group stays in the flow of the list.

When not

  • To say something about a screen that still works: take the notice band. A state block replaces the content, a notice sits beside it.
  • For a wait: take the skeleton card. A skeleton mirrors the real row, so the wait promises what arrives.

Limits

Usage rules that name this component. Each is written once, on Architecture, the usage rules, with where it was taken from; this is the short copy.

  • R3. A .state never shares a screen with .toolbar, .selbar or .actionbar: there is nothing to filter, select or save on an empty or failed screen.
  • R6. Exactly one h1 on a screen, and it names the screen. A state title is an h2, except on 8.0, 8.1 and 8.2, where the state is the screen.
