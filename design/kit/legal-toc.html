How to use it

When

At the head of a long document, so it is navigable without a rail. On 9.0, 9.1 and 9.2 it lists the sections of the policy and jumps to them.

When not

  • For navigation between pages: take the app header on a desktop and the bottom tabs on a phone inside the product, or the breadcrumb for the way back. The contents box only moves within one legal document.
  • For a link inside a sentence: take .link.

Behaviour at width

Fluid, no threshold (Stage 10 Step 4). The list is a flex row that wraps (flex-wrap: wrap) and each link keeps its 44px target, so in a narrow place the links run on to further lines and nothing is cut. No query, and the CSS did not change in Stage 10; it was already fluid. The same contents box, live, in two places:
