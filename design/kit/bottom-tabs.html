What stands here is the tabs, not the bar. .bottomtabs is display: none above 820px by its own rule, and this page is wider than that, so the four .btab links sit on the hero's own row, with the hero's 12px gap, which the bar does not have. The bar with its hairline, its surface ground and its sticky bottom edge is under Other forms and appears when the window is narrowed to 820. In place: 2.0 Synthesis at 360, or the frame on Screen shell.

Variants and states

The states belong to each tab: rest, focus and current. Current takes the accent as ink on the glyph and the label at once. There is no hover, no pressed tab and no disabled tab, because bottom-tabs.css paints none of them. The bar has one form, so the variant axis is the viewport, and that one cannot be forced inside a cell.

How to use it

When

At the foot of every signed-in screen, as the last zone of the screen shell, and seen at 820px and below only. Canonical global 0.1, the same as the header it replaces.

The same destinations as the header, plus Account. On a wide screen the account is the avatar in the corner; on a narrow one it is the fourth tab.

When not

  • Above 820: the navigation is the app header's text tabs. Both are in every screen's markup and the media rules choose.
  • On a screen read by somebody who is not signed in (0.0, 6.4, 9.x): no tabs at all. A shared brief has no navigation to offer (the public bar).
