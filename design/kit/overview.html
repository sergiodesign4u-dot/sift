Stage 08 · Tokens + Components

The Sift design system

Two levels of tokens and a file per component, split out of the flat kit that Stage 07 built. The values did not change when they moved: every one of them arrived from the approved brand plate at Stage 06 and carries its origin comment to this day, which is the whole chain from a pixel somebody chose to a token a component reads.

The foundations come first and they come before the components, because this is the cheapest place in the stage to change anything. A change to the type scale or a radius here costs one file and reaches every screen through var(); the same change after the components exist costs thirty files plus thirty pages that already show the old value.
