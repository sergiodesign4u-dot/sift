How to use it

When

One feedback item under a theme, on 4.0 and 4.1, one per item, grouped by source. The snippet links to 5.0, the full item in context.

The citation number is a two-way tie. The same number is what the summary chip points at, so a reader can go claim to item and item back to claim.

Author references are scrubbed by default, and the snippet shows the scrubbed form.

When not

  • For the theme itself, ranked: take the theme card. A zone set that differs is a different component.
  • For the claim that the items support: that is the summary paragraph of 4.0, with linked citation chips.
  • For an item that cannot be shown at all: that is 5.1, a page that says what was withheld and why, not a snippet with an empty quote.
