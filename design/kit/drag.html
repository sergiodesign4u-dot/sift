When to use it

On the composer, and nowhere else. It is the only place in this product where a person reorders anything, and it is there because the order is the recommendation: 6.1 says so in the toolbar, in the words "Order = recommended priority".

It stays secondary ink because it is a grip and not a decision. The decision is the order it produces.
