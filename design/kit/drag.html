Variants and states

Not drawn by state: the handle has none that paints. Its only states are the cursor, grab at rest and grabbing while pressed, and a cursor cannot be shown in a table. No hover, pressed or focus row is drawn because drag.css paints none of them: no hover colour, no active colour, no focus rule. One form, so the matrix is the handle alone and the handle in its row.

How to use it

When

On the composer, and nowhere else. It is the only place in this product where a person reorders anything, and it is there because the order is the recommendation: 6.1 says so in the toolbar, in the words "Order = recommended priority".

It stays secondary ink because it is a grip and not a decision. The decision is the order it produces.

When not

  • To let a person remove a row: take .btn--icon. Remove is an action with a name. Dragging is a gesture.
  • To show a rank: take .rank. The handle changes the order. The rank reports it.
  • To reorder anything outside the composer: there is no such place. If one appears, it uses this handle rather than a new one.
