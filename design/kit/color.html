Semantic

Roles. Each one points at a primitive through var() and says how many places it was read from. The number is what makes the rule "one single use is not yet a role" checkable at a glance. The prefix carries the surface: --text- is ink, --bg- is fill, --line- is line, and a role may not paint two of them.

Both themes, side by side

A pair is a property of the level, not an event, so it is shown as material rather than as the current position of the switch. The right half of every card is forced to the light theme whatever the switch says. The pair is deliberately not a mirror: contrast is measured against the opposite ground, so the light theme takes a different step of the ramp.

Contrast, every role, both themes

Tabulated for ALL roles and not only the ones somebody called textual: a fill role placed on a small bold caption passes as a surface at 3:1 and fails as text at 4.5:1, and a table of text and background pairs never sees it, because nobody declared it text. Ink is measured at 4.5:1, or at 3:1 from 24px and from 19px bold. Fill and line are measured at 3:1, WCAG 1.4.11. A hairline that separates rather than identifies is exempt, and the verdict column says so.
