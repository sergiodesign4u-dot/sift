Variants and states

Not interactive itself: the bar has no hover, focus or pressed state. Its buttons are the button atom and keep their states on Button. One axis, the trigger. Hidden and present are not states of the bar: they are the shell's trigger, which shows the on-selection form and hides the head action.

How to use it

When

On a list where the person selects: the bar appears only when at least one item is selected, carries the count, and holds the next move. While it is up the head action is hidden, so the screen never shows two primary actions.

On the composer: the bar is always there. Nothing is being selected, the artifact IS the selection. Same component, one declared trigger variant, not a second bar.

When not

  • For the closing actions of a form or a flow with nothing selected: take the action bar.
  • For the one action of a screen at rest: that is the head action in the screen head, which this bar replaces while it is up.
