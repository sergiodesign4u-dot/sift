Variants and states

Not interactive itself: hover and focus belong to the input and the button, see input.html and button.html. One appearance, four purposes, and the purpose changes what is inside the form, not the form. The states are the inventory's: rest, submitting [?], error. Submitting is not painted by any rule in the system, so it is not shown. Error is a line under the field (field.html) and has no copy in any screen [?].

How to use it

When

When the person types and submits. The form is the wrapper that gives the fields their rhythm and nothing else, so everything a form needs comes from a component that already exists: the field, the label, the control, the buttons.

When not

  • To restyle a field or a button for one purpose: a difference written beside the form is an undeclared variant. Add the variant to the component first.
  • For a column mapping or a file drop: those are one-off blocks, not a form.
