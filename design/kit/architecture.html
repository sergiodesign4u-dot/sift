Two ladders, and they are not the same ladder

Both use the word "level", which is exactly why they get separated here. One says where a VALUE comes from. The other says what CONTAINS what.

the ladder of tokens

↓
SEMANTIC  a role, through var()

Two levels, not three. A component token is a third layer of renaming that buys no flexibility, so it appears only where a state lands on no role at all. Nothing here has needed one yet.

the ladder of components
atoms  contain nothing from the kit
↓

↓

↓
patterns  Stage 09, not before

Three is the ceiling. The deepest nesting here is a dialog holding a form, and a form is already an organism, so both stay at level three. Nesting decides the ORDER inside a level, not a fourth number.

Grouping by purpose is forbidden. By purpose a button and a login dialog are both "forms" and land side by side, though one lives inside the other. The cost is not aesthetic: an @import sorted alphabetically puts dialog.css before field.css, so the composed thing sits above its own parts in the cascade, and the first contextual fix reaches for !important.

Three surfaces, and a role paints exactly one

The prefix carries the surface. A role may not paint two of them even at the same value, because the thresholds differ, and that is not pedantry: a fill role placed on a 12px bold caption passes as a surface at 3:1 and fails as text at 4.5:1, and a table of text and background pairs never catches it, because nobody declared it text.

PrefixSurfaceFloorExample
--text-*ink: text, icon glyph4.5:1, or 3:1 from 24px or 19px bold--text-secondary
--bg-*fill: page, card, band, badge3:1, WCAG 1.4.11--bg-action
--line-*line: border, divider, focus ring3:1 where the line identifies a control--line-control

This is why the type scale is --type-* and not --text-*: one prefix, one meaning, checkable by grep. Two roles get two tokens even when the value is identical today, and the test is not the colour but the question "can these two places ever part": --bg-action and --text-evidence are both the one cyan, and attribute A2 signed the citation separately from the action.

A difference written as a contextual override is not a patch, it is an undeclared variant. Either it is a variant, and it gets a modifier and a row in the inventory, or it is drift, and it folds into the nearest declared form. Two of them were caught that way at the Stage 07 critique and promoted to .input--mono and .input--dense.
