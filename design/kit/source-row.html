Variants and states

One axis: emphasis, healthy or failed. A failed row carries the alert tag (dashed, so it cannot read as a healthy one), a row-level alert line that says in words what happened, and its own retry. Hover is the row's own rule, --bg-hover. Focus is shown on the button inside, because the row itself is not focusable. There is no pressed row: source-row.css paints none. The tag, the icon slot and the buttons keep their own states on their own pages: Status tag, Icon slot, Button.

How to use it

When

A connected source on 3.0 and 3.5: what it is, how much it holds, when it last arrived, whether PII is scrubbed, and the one way to manage it.

The alert line under a failed row explains the state in words and offers the retry, because a tag alone says something is wrong and nothing about what to do. The voice rule is that every state names the next move.

When not

  • For a source that is not connected yet: take the source card.
  • For a saved brief or a composer row: take the saved brief row or the composer row. The inventory keeps seven row-shaped components apart because their zones differ.
  • For a destination in a hub: take the list row link.

Icon, name, stats, status, action, in reading order. Kept apart from the saved brief row and the composer row: the inventory calls them three components and not variants, because whole zones are gone between them (design/kit/docs/inventory.md, "Level 2, molecules").
