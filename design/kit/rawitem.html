How to use it

When

One feedback item on its own page, the end of the chain theme, items, raw source: 5.0, and the meta line of its state 5.1.

The words are the evidence. This is what a PM pulls up when a decision is challenged, so the quote is set to be read aloud, at the document reading step and the full measure.

When not

  • For an item in a list under a theme, linking to its page: take the evidence snippet. Quote first, a strip with a hover; a different component.
  • For the facts about the item (source, author, which theme it supports): take the provenance list under it.
  • For a customer quote on a theme row: that is the recessed quote of the theme card, smaller and secondary.
