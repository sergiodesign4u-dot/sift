Live. Shaped like the theme card, then the generic card

The wait, laid out as what arrives. The shimmer belongs to the skeleton bar atom, and it stops under prefers-reduced-motion.

Variants and states

Not interactive: no hover, focus or pressed state. A skeleton is a promise about the shape of what is coming, and nothing in it can be acted on. Two shapes; the rows below are the content forms the colour screens carry. The bars inside keep their own page: Skeleton bar.

How to use it

When

While content that has a known shape is on its way: the ranked themes on 2.2, the evidence items on 4.2, the link row on 6.3.

Shape it like what arrives, so the wait promises the right thing. Where the theme card arrives, take .skelrow: the IA asks for skeleton rows shaped like the real theme card, label, quote, provenance.

When not

  • For anything already known: the count, the recency, the rank, a title, an explanation. Show it as itself, never as a grey bar.
  • For the progress of a process with named phases: take the phase strip. On 2.2 it stands above the skeleton and says which phase is running.

Anatomy

Two shapes, each a container for the bar atom. The shape is the component; the bars are content.

ZoneClass or elementRule
the row.skelrowThe theme card's grid: the same 22px and 20px tracks, the same gaps, the same 13px padding and the same rule underneath, so nothing shifts when the real row lands
the box.skelrow .skel-boxSpans the checkbox and rank tracks. A flat surface, no shimmer
the body.skelrow .skel-bodyThree bars on a 9px gap: label, quote, provenance
the card.skelcardA stack of bars on an 8px gap under a hairline
the bars.skel.a, .skel.b, .skel.cThe atom, with its shimmer and its three widths

Axes, with the rule that chooses

AxisValuesThe rule that chooses
shapegeneric rows 1 · shaped like the theme card 1Shaped like what arrives. Where a theme card arrives, the row skeleton; where text of no fixed shape arrives, the generic card

The counts are the inventory's, in screens. Measured in design/: 6 theme-card rows on 2.2; 5 generic list cards and 1 head card on 4.2; 1 two-line card on 6.3, which the inventory does not list.

Kept off the scale, byte for byte, because no primitive equals them: the grid tracks 22px and 20px, the 13px vertical padding of the row, the 16px height of the box and the 9px gap of the body. The shimmer reads --bg-surface, --bg-raised and --ease through the bar atom, not through this file.
