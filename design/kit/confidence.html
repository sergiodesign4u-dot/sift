Variants and states

Not interactive: no hover, focus or pressed state. No states, and that is a decision rather than an omission. The confidence indicator is information, not a control. It cannot be hovered, focused, pressed or disabled, and giving it a hover would tell the person it does something. Four states are given to a component because it is interactive, not because a template has four cells. Faking one with a class the product does not carry (.is-hover) would be the same defect as an invented role. So the matrix is the signal levels: count only 15 · count plus badge 9.

How to use it

When

Beside every theme, everywhere a theme appears: the ranked list, the detail head, the composer row, and the public brief a stakeholder reads without a login. The last one matters most. A brief that dropped the count on the way out would be the product breaking its own promise at exactly the moment somebody outside is checking it.

The low-signal badge is the product saying a thing it would be easier not to say. Thin evidence is never dressed up as a strong signal, and on 6.2 the product goes further: it lets the author rank a four-mention theme second and labels it anyway, because the order is the author's judgement and the count is a fact.

When not

  • To show a status the person can act on: take .tag. A status is about the source; confidence is about the evidence.
  • To show how much of the corpus a view covers: take .scope. The scope line is the denominator of the screen; the indicator is the count of one theme.
  • To add a bar or a percentage: take nothing. Rank and n=X already encode volume twice. A third encoding of one quantity is decoration, which A1 forbids, and it was dropped for exactly that reason at Stage 07.
