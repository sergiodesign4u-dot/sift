Confidence indicator

Mandatory in MVP by founder decision D2, and the transparency condition the riskiest assumption of this product depends on. It is not a feature that can be traded away: without it the synthesis is a claim, and with it the synthesis is a claim with its size attached.

When to use it

Beside every theme, everywhere a theme appears: the ranked list, the detail head, the composer row, and the public brief a stakeholder reads without a login. The last one matters most. A brief that dropped the count on the way out would be the product breaking its own promise at exactly the moment somebody outside is checking it.

The low-signal badge is the product saying a thing it would be easier not to say. Thin evidence is never dressed up as a strong signal, and on 6.2 the product goes further: it lets the author rank a four-mention theme second and labels it anyway, because the order is the author's judgement and the count is a fact.

States

No states, and that is a decision rather than an omission. The confidence indicator is information, not a control. It cannot be hovered, focused, pressed or disabled, and giving it a hover would tell the person it does something.

Four states are given to a component because it is interactive, not because a template has four cells. Faking one with a class the product does not carry (.is-hover) would be the same defect as an invented role.
