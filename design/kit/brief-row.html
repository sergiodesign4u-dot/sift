How to use it

When

On 6.0, to list the briefs a PM has saved: Shared ones and Drafts, each with the one way in. The meta line says what the person needs to know before opening: how many themes, how much evidence, and whether anybody outside can see it.

When not

  • For the rows of the brief being composed: take the composer row. It has a handle and a remove and no status.
  • For a connected source: take the source row.
  • For a hub destination: take the list row link.

Anatomy

Kept apart from the other row-shaped components: the inventory calls them seven components and not one with variants, because the zone sets differ and whole zones are gone (design/kit/docs/inventory.md, "Level 2, molecules"). Zones in reading order: title, meta, status, actions.

ZoneClass or elementRule
title.brief-titleThe anchor and the way in. 14px, medium weight, underlined on hover
hit area.brief-title::afterA -12px overlay across the title zone. It is -12 and not the grey layer's -11: at this type size -11 lands the target on 43px, one pixel under the 44px floor
meta.brief-meta12px secondary ink, 1.5 line height
status.tagShared or Draft, the tag atom
actions.brief-actionsThe outlined button, Open or Continue

valueshared 2 · draft 1The status is a word in the tag and a fact in the meta line. A draft's line says there is no link yet
