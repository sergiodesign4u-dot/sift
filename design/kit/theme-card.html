How to use it

When

One theme in the ranked list of 2.0, in signal-rank order. The whole card links to 4.0 Theme detail; the select control adds the theme to a brief.

The trust chain starts before the click. The quote stands for the cluster, which is what separates a list of labels from evidence at a glance.

The rank is written down so the ranking claim is quotable and re-checkable (A4).

When not

  • For a theme inside the brief composer: take the composer brief row. Handle and remove instead of checkbox and drill; a zone that is gone is a different component.
  • For one feedback item under a theme: take the evidence snippet.
  • For a destination with no data on it: take the list row link.
