How to use it

When

On 0.0, to walk the path the person takes inside the app. Where a step makes a claim the product can prove, the proof is the product's own component, not a picture of it.

When not

  • To show a plan: take the tier card.
  • To show the progress of a flow: that is the phase strip, not a marketing step.
  • To prove a claim with an invented figure: the number comes from the product (principle 5, traceable or it does not exist).
