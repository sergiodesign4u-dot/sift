Variants and states

The shell is not interactive itself: it has no hover, focus or pressed state, and its zones keep their own states on their own pages (App header, Bottom tabs, App footer). One axis, the neighbour: a screen that is the page, or a screen shown on a stand. What changes inside it is the selection trigger, which is a rule of the shell on its children. The bar's note is the screen's own static text.

How to use it

When

.app for every product screen. A screen fills its canvas: header, main, footer, bottom tabs, nothing around it. A colour screen is a copy of a wireframe, and a wireframe is a real screen, not an exhibit.

.appframe for a stand only, the kit and the concept, where a screen is shown as an exhibit. Same zones, different neighbour.

A selection bar and a head action on the same screen are mutually exclusive, and the shell is what enforces it.

When not

  • Do not frame a product screen. Two kinds of screen in one folder is how a reviewer learns to distrust the folder.
  • Do not show the selection bar or hide the head action from inside a molecule: the trigger is the shell's, and the selection bar and the screen head leave it out on purpose.
  • Do not drop :only-child from the centring rules: an inline state inside a populated list would lay the whole main out as a row.

Anatomy

Five zones top to bottom. Three are organisms with their own pages, one is a molecule placed by the shell, and the main region is the shell's own.

ZoneClass or elementRule
header.appbar or .public-barOrganisms with their own files. 52px, the first child
notice, optional.notice as a direct childThe molecule, given the 22px gutter by this file, 16px at 820 and below
main.appmainTakes the free height (flex: 1), 20px by 22px padding. Centres a lone state block or dialog
footer.appfoot or .pubfootOrganisms with their own files
bottom tabs.bottomtabsThe organism. Last, sticky, drawn at 820 and below

Axes, with the rule that chooses

AxisValuesThe rule that chooses
neighbour.app 22 colour screens · .appframe 0 product screens, 2 standsSame zones, different neighbour. A screen that is the page takes .app; a screen shown as an exhibit takes .appframe
headerapp header 20 · public bar 2, in colourSigned in or not. A reader with no session has nothing to navigate to
selectionnothing checked · a checkbox checkedThe trigger reads ANY checked .check in the shell. It fires on 2.0 when a theme is ticked, and also on 3.2 and 7.2, which hold checked settings boxes but no selection bar and no head action, so nothing moves there [?]
centringa lone dialog: 6.3 and its generating state · a lone state block: none in colour:only-child, never a class on the main

The counts are read from the markup of design/*.html. The inventory gives the shell 27 screens and two forms, and calls the pair an axis, not two components.
