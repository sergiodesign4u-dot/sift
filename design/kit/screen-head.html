How to use it

When

At the top of every screen of the app. It names where the person is, says in one line what the screen is for, and carries the one action the screen offers at rest. A ranked screen also carries its denominator under it, because a ranking with no denominator cannot be checked.

Which form. .screenhead, bordered, when the head carries a scope line or an action, or opens a working surface: the lists of 2.0, 3.0, 4.0 and 6.0 with their states, and the brief being built on 6.1 and 6.2 (15 colour screens). .main-head, plain, when the head only names the page: a settings page (7.0, 7.2), a step of a flow under a breadcrumb (3.2), an item to read (5.0). Counted on the colour screens at Stage 09 Step 6.

When not

  • To hold a second primary action: Design Principle 4 allows exactly one per screen. A second one belongs in the selection bar or the action bar.
  • To hold the synthesis paragraph: that is .summary, a different component.
  • To hold filters: they go in the toolbar under the head.

Limits

Usage rules that name this component. Each is written once, on Architecture, the usage rules, with where it was taken from; this is the short copy.

  • R1. At most one filled .btn visible on a screen; every other real action takes .btn--ghost. An action at rest and an action on selection are exclusive in CSS: a ticked theme card shows .selbar and hides .head-action. On 0.0 Home the count is per section.
  • R6. Exactly one h1 on a screen, and it names the screen. A state title is an h2, except where the state is the whole screen: 8.0, 8.1, 8.2 and 5.1.
