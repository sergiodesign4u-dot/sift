How to use it

When

Wherever a person types one value: log in, sign up, reset, the profile, the brief title. The wrapper is what keeps a label and its control one unit with one rhythm under it, so a form of several fields does not need a spacing rule on the form.

When not

  • For a control that is a column of a grid, such as the mapping grid on 3.2: those are dense inputs with a name from aria-label, and a label above each would stack the grid.
  • To show an error as a red border: input.css paints none, and the line under the field is the declared form.
  • To put two controls in one field: one field is one value.
Do
A label above the control
The name is on screen after the placeholder is gone, and it is read by a screen reader.
Do not
The placeholder as the only label
It leaves when the person starts typing, and the field no longer says what it holds. The label atom exists for this.
