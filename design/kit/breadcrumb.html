How to use it

When

On a screen below another one: a theme under Synthesis, a CSV upload under Sources, evidence under a theme. It is the way back, one line, never the way forward. The first item always leads to the section the person came from.

When not

  • On a top-level screen: Synthesis, Sources and Briefs are the three navigation entries and stand at the top.
  • To show a step in a process: that is the phase strip.
  • On the legal screens: they carry .legal-crumb, a separate block.
