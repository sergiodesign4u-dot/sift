How to use it

When

Above a ranked or evidence list, where the person narrows by source, time or confidence and sees how the list is sorted. The facets carry counts, which is why the toolbar is absent from 2.1 and 2.2: an empty or loading screen has no counts to show.

When not

  • Before a first run: with no data every facet would read zero. The empty screens drop it.
  • To hold an action: the one action of a screen lives in the screen head.
  • To mark the active facet with the accent: the active chip steps up a surface instead, see Filter chip.
