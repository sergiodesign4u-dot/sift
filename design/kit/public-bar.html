When and when not

When

At the top of a screen read by somebody who is not signed in: the marketing home, the legal pages, auth, the shared brief. The bar says whose page it is and nothing more.

When not

  • Inside the application: the app header carries the three entries and the account.
  • To hold an action: the public document has no actions, and principle 4 keeps one primary action per screen.
  • To hold navigation: .nav-links has no screen. It was removed from the product layer at Stage 07.

Where it stands

Two screens in colour. The inventory counts 10 in grey: 0.0, 1.0 (three screens), 6.4, 8.1, 8.2, 9.0, 9.1, 9.2.
