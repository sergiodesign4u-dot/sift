How Sift works

Three steps from scattered signal to a call you can stand behind. It mirrors the path you take inside the app.

How to use it

When

On the one screen read by somebody who has not signed in and has not decided to read it: the marketing home. It has a wider measure and a larger first line because of that (the comment above the rules in kit.css).

When not

  • Inside the application: .mk-h1 at 36px is for 0.0 alone, and so is the large button.
  • For prose: the shared brief is a reading column at 720px, not a section stack.
  • For a trust block or a footer link block: those are one-offs and are not in this file.

Where it stands

Only 0.0 Home, in colour. The inventory counts 1 screen, in grey as well. On photography, the inventory row says "See Findings 4": 0.0 is the one place the concept allows a hero photo, the wireframe has no image block, and Step 4 has two options (add the block upstream, or record that the product carries none). The decision is the owner's and is not made here.

Behaviour at width

The wide column and an auto-fit grid (Stage 10 Step 4, round 3). .public-main--wide is --container-page (65rem, the same 1040px). .mk-grid is auto-fit from a 13.75rem (220px) minimum, guarded by min(13.75rem, 100%) so a place under the minimum still holds one full column; it reads its place, not the viewport. The census asked for --grid-col-min (15rem) here and it was withdrawn on verification: at 15rem the fourth tier drops to a row of its own at a 1440 window (4 x 240 + 3 gaps is 996px against a 992px column), so the tier grid keeps the minimum its four cards were drawn at.
