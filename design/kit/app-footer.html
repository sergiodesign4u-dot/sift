How to use it

When

At the foot of every product frame, as the injected global 0.2. The line is the product's data promise, kept in view on every screen: customer feedback carries PII, it is scrubbed by default, and that default has to be visible (CLAUDE.md, "Compliance is day one").

When not

  • As a second action area: principle 4 says the screen has one primary action and the footer does not compete with it.
  • For a marketing link block: 0.0 composes .mk-footer with it, and that block is a one-off, not a variant of this one.
  • On the public document: 6.4 has its own closing line, .pubfoot, which belongs to the public document.
