How to use it

When

Beside every item in an ordered list the product asks somebody to defend: the ranked themes on 2.0, the rows of a brief on 6.1, the key takeaways in the public document.

On the composer it does a second job: the order of those rows IS the recommendation, so the number is the artifact rather than a decoration on it.

When not

  • To show how strong an item is: take .conf. Rank is position. Confidence is size. Both are on the row, and they say different things.
  • To number a step in a process: take .step-n of the phase strip. A phase number is a place in a sequence you move through.
  • To label a group: take .group-t.
