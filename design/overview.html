Stage 07 · UI + Visual

All screens, in colour

The product language applied to real screens. Each one is a copy of its grey wireframe that owns the visual layer and nothing else: the structure, the text and the set of states stay the property of wireframes/, which is frozen. Every screen here is assembled from the component kit and carries no style of its own.

Not the whole product, and the map below says so. This stage paints a sample chosen for how much of the component inventory it covers, and the rest is assembled once at Stage 12, on a system that by then has patterns, breakpoints and motion. Building a screen three times is how a design system becomes a set of three disagreeing screens.

Critique, and what it changed

Three instruments, run independently and deduped afterwards: a browser walking every screen at a measured 360 and at 1440, a scripted audit of contrast and target size over all 23 pages, and Codex reading the source read-only. The browser and the script own what only renders; Codex owns what only reading catches. Every finding was re-checked against the file before it was fixed, and one was withdrawn on verification.

Found byFindingVerdict
Browser, 360The drag handle was 300px wide. An svg with no width is 300x150 by specification, and every icon slot in the kit sized its own icon except this one, so the composer ran off a 360 screenFixed in the kit. Then every icon in the product was measured: none renders over 40px

Browser, pixel diff.list-foot was a block holding an inline-flex button, so the line box added a pixel under the listFixed: it is a flex container
Script, targetsThree controls under the 44px floor: the breadcrumb link at 15px, the mapping-grid select at 40px, and the heading link in the trust list at 17pxAll three fixed in the kit. The bar is now exactly as tall as the thing you aim at
Script, contrastEvery piece of text on every screen measured against its composited backgroundNo finding: nothing falls below AA
CodexP1. The four seed screens used a class .lbl that the kit never declared. Its only declaration was inside the stand's own style block, which is exactly the "assembled past the kit" defectRemoved from the markup. The other twelve screens never had it
CodexP2. Two contextual overrides of the input, .linkrow .input and .src-map .input: an undeclared variant eachBoth promoted to declared variants, .input--mono and .input--dense, and named in the inventory

Kit and tokens

One click from the product to the system it was assembled from. A screen that needs something the kit does not have gets it added to the kit first and used second, which is the whole reason the kit exists.

Component kit Fifty components in three levels, every token alive with its origin, and the inventory of the whole product.
